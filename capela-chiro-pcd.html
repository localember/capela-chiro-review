Payment options

PCD discount plan

Save on care with a PCD plan

Preferred Chiropractic Doctor (PCD) is a discount medical plan our office uses so cash-paying patients can save on care. It is not insurance. Here are a few things you should know.

  • PCD plans guarantee members save 25% off standard fees for exams, x-rays, adjustments, and therapies with participating PCD providers in America.
  • PCD plans include a spouse and legal dependents up to age 26 at no additional charge (married children excluded).
  • PCD is a 365-day yearly plan. It expires on the same day each year, not at the end of the calendar year.
  • PCD is not insurance, and is void in conjunction with any third-party entity or federal or state health insurance programs, including Workers’ Compensation.
  • PCD plan members cannot file claims on services rendered at PCD rates, and cannot apply those visits toward an insurance deductible or use PCD as secondary insurance.

As a Preferred Chiropractic Doctor provider, we can give PCD plan members 25% off exams, x-rays, adjustments, and therapies for a whole year.
